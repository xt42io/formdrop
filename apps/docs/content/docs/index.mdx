FormDrop gives an HTML form somewhere to post to. You do not write a backend,
host an endpoint, or manage a database — you point the form at a URL and the
submissions arrive in a dashboard, your inbox, Slack, Discord, or a
spreadsheet.

## The whole thing

```html
<form action="https://api.formdrop.co/f/your-form-slug" method="POST">
  <input name="email" type="email" required />
  <textarea name="message"></textarea>
  <button>Send</button>
</form>
```

That is a working form. No JavaScript, no build step, no key.

## What you get

- **A dashboard** for every submission, searchable and exportable.
- **Notifications** by email, Slack and Discord the moment something arrives.
- **Sync** to Google Sheets and Airtable.
- **An API** for reading your data back, and [SDKs](/sdks/javascript) if you
  would rather not write `fetch` by hand.
